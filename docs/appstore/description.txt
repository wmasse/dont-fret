Learn guitar by playing real songs. Don't Fret listens to your guitar through your Mac's microphone and tells you when you've got it right.

PLAY REAL SONGS
80+ songs from folk classics to Britpop, each rated for difficulty. Play along in time, use Wait Mode (the song waits until you play the right chord), or follow an auto-scrolling chord sheet. Loop the hard part and it speeds up as you get it.

HEAR HOW IT SHOULD SOUND
Hear the Chords plays the whole song with a realistic guitar: strummed, fingerpicked, boom-chick country, palm-muted power chords or reggae, at the song's own tempo. Change the key, add a capo and see every chord shape.

ADD ANY SONG
Search for a song and Don't Fret finds a chord chart for the whole song, then listens to the recording to confirm the key, tempo, capo and strumming pattern. No chart? It works the chords out from the song's preview and tells you so. You can also paste a chord chart, import ChordPro files, or build a song chord by chord.

RIFFS & SOLOS
18 riffs, solos, melodies and exercises in TAB. Hear them on acoustic, clean electric or overdriven electric, slow them down, loop them, then play them back to the mic note by note.

BUILD REAL SKILLS
• Chord Library: 91 shapes from first chords to jazz voicings
• Chord Trainer: chords flash up and you play them; your weak ones come back more often
• 1-Minute Changes and a Strum Trainer for rhythm
• Tuner and chord detector
• XP, levels, streaks, a daily challenge and a learning path for your level

STUDIO
Record yourself as audio or as video with sound, including from an iPhone through Continuity Camera. Play along to a click or one of your songs, then trim, cut out any part, loop, and stack layers like a second guitar or vocals. Pick a sound preset or adjust bass, treble, reverb and fades, and export a finished .m4a, .wav or .mp4.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no tracking and no ads. Your progress and recordings stay on your Mac. The app only goes online to fetch album covers and when you search for a song to add.

Works with any acoustic guitar, or an electric through an amp or audio interface. The built-in Mac microphone works fine.
